How it’s deployed

Two ways to run it.
Our servers or yours.

01 · Silow-hosted
Fastest start
  • Install the recorder and go. No infrastructure work on your side.
  • Redaction runs on our servers. Raw captures are deleted within 48 hours; only derived patterns are kept.
  • Silow is your data processor under a DPA.
  • Subprocessors: a major cloud provider (ISO 27001 / 27017 / 27018) and model inference with zero data retention, no training on your data.
02 · On your servers
Everything inside your perimeter
  • One Docker Compose package, an install README, and an acceptance script that checks every stage.
  • SSO / SAML and audit logs.
  • Models on your GPUs, your cloud account, or a zero-data-retention provider you choose.
  • Screenshots and video never reach Silow.
Start hosted, move to your servers later · same recorder, same reports
